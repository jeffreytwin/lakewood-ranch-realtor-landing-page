Get Help With My Build
Can I use an agent if I want to build?
Yes. The builder pays our commission, so you get expert help at no cost to you.
Will using an agent raise my price?
No. Every builder has an established agent program. Using us does not change your price or your negotiation.
Do I need to bring you to my first builder visit?
In most cases, yes. Many builders want you to register your agent at your first visit. Reach out before you tour model homes.
What if I buy a resale home instead?
You pay nothing up front. Our fee is handled at closing.
